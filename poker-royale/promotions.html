Bonus Terms & Conditions

Important information about all promotions

How do I claim the Welcome Bonus?
Simply create a new account, make your first deposit of at least PKR 1,000 via any payment method, and the 200% bonus will be automatically credited to your bonus balance. No promo code required.
What are the wagering requirements?
Most bonuses have a 5x wagering requirement, meaning you need to generate 5x the bonus amount in rake before the bonus converts to real cash. For example, a PKR 10,000 bonus requires PKR 50,000 in rake. VIP players enjoy reduced wagering.
Can I withdraw bonus funds?
Bonus funds cannot be withdrawn directly. They must first be converted to real balance by meeting the wagering requirements. Once converted, you can withdraw freely via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or bank transfer.
Do bonuses expire?
Yes, most bonuses expire 30 days after being claimed if the wagering requirements are not met. Daily login rewards expire after 24 hours if not claimed. VIP tier benefits reset quarterly.
How does the referral program work?
Share your unique referral link. When a friend signs up using your link and makes a minimum deposit of PKR 1,000, both you and your friend receive PKR 1,000 bonus each. There is no limit to how many friends you can refer!
